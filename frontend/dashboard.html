Why this is not a validated prediction yet

Conditions, the 72-hour outlook, NOAA ENSO context and historical patterns support situational awareness, but they do not yet make this a validated prediction service.

  • Weather comes from provider estimates; no local station observations are attached.
  • The heat screen and rain, wind and cold thresholds are illustrative, not calibrated probabilities or official warnings.
  • Historical values are also provider estimates, so they cannot validate earlier forecasts against observed weather.
  • The page refreshes every 15 minutes, but provider update timing and the data timestamp determine how recent the weather is.

What would make predictions trustworthy

Collect timestamped forecasts and local observations over time, compare each forecast with what happened, and report accuracy and uncertainty by location and forecast lead time. Then add multiple forecast sources, fallback handling and alerts backed by measured performance.

Screening thresholds and limits

The screen uses the peak rolling 24-hour period in the 3-day forecast and project thresholds: rainfall ≥50/100 mm, sustained wind ≥40/60 km/h, and forecast low ≤16/8/5°C. These are not official warning criteria; rainfall is not a flood prediction, and forecast wind does not include gusts.
